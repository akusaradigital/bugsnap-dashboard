# Anti-Slop UI Audit #001 (2026-10-13)

Project: `bugsnap-dashboard`
Auditor: Anti-Slop UI Filter (Mode 2 — AFTER)
Framework: Next.js 14 / Tailwind CSS

---

## Findings Summary

This audit evaluates all components and pages across `bugsnap-dashboard` against Anti-Slop standards (Hard Gate, Purpose-Gate, and Quality Locks) as well as the workspace `CLAUDE.md`.

| No | Category / Rule | Target File | Priority | Brief Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **R-18 (Testimonials)** | `src/components/site/TestimonialWall.tsx`, `src/app/page.tsx` | **HIGH** | Fictitious testimonials with artificial names, roles, and reviews ("Arya Kusuma", "Marcus Chen", etc.) violate the Hard Gate on social proof authenticity. |
| 2 | **R-02 (Copywriting)** | `src/lib/i18n/marketing.ts`, `src/app/(app)/compare/page.tsx`, `src/app/features/FeaturesContent.tsx`, `src/app/how-it-works/HowItWorksContent.tsx`, `src/app/solutions/SolutionsContent.tsx`, `src/app/pricing/PricingToggle.tsx` | **HIGH** | Use of em dashes (`—`) in UI copy and marketing text is strictly forbidden; must be replaced with commas, periods, colons, parentheses, or hyphens `-`. |
| 3 | **R-38 & i18n Rule** | `src/app/features/FeaturesContent.tsx`, `src/app/how-it-works/HowItWorksContent.tsx`, `src/app/page.tsx` | **HIGH** | Hardcoded English strings directly in JSX (bullet points, badges, metric labels) without i18n keys (`useT()`). |
| 4 | **R-01 (Color & Gradients)** | `src/components/StaticShell.tsx`, `src/app/page.tsx`, `src/app/v/[id]/page.tsx` | **MEDIUM** | Radial glow orbs (`sOrb1`, `heroOrb1`, blur blobs) in hero backgrounds violate prohibitions on generic decorative AI gradient blobs without structural hierarchy. |
| 5 | **R-07 (Background Texture)** | `src/components/StaticShell.tsx` | **MEDIUM** | Repeating grid square background patterns (`backgroundSize: 48px 48px`) in static shells are generic AI defaults that do not serve brand identity. |
| 6 | **R-19 (Motion & Loops)** | `src/components/StaticShell.tsx`, `src/app/page.tsx`, `src/app/admin/layout.tsx` | **MEDIUM** | Infinite rotation/floating keyframe animations (`sOrb1 10s infinite`, `heroOrb1 10s infinite`, unprompted pulsing dots) introduce perpetual motion visual noise. |
| 7 | **R-04 (Generic AI Icons)** | `src/app/features/FeaturesContent.tsx`, `src/app/extension/ExtensionContent.tsx` | **MEDIUM** | Lightning bolt icons (`IconBolt`, `lucide:zap`) used as generic decorative filler without concrete functional relevance. |
| 8 | **R-16 (AI Buzzwords)** | `src/lib/i18n/marketing.ts`, `src/app/about/page.tsx` | **LOW** | Clichéd AI marketing buzzwords like "effortless" and "seamlessly"; replace with concrete, benefit-driven language. |
| 9 | **R-15 (Generic CTAs)** | `src/lib/i18n/marketing.ts`, `src/app/features/FeaturesContent.tsx` | **LOW** | Generic template CTA labels ("Get Started Free", "Explore Features", "Get Started in 30 Seconds"); replace with specific action-oriented CTAs. |
| 10 | **R-01 & Solid Colors** | `src/app/features/FeaturesContent.tsx`, `src/app/how-it-works/HowItWorksContent.tsx`, `src/app/solutions/SolutionsContent.tsx` | **LOW** | Heavy background gradients (`bg-gradient-to-b from-site-surface via-site-surface to-site-surface-2/60`) on feature cards instead of clean solid token colors per CLAUDE.md. |
| 11 | **R-31 & Status Dots** | `src/app/admin/layout.tsx`, `src/app/admin/supabase/page.tsx` | **LOW** | Continuous `animate-pulse` on static green status dots ("Supabase: Online") without state transition triggers. |
